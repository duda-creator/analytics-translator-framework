Usage guidance
✓ Do
  • Use dark variant on dark or navy backgrounds
  • Use light variant on white or light grey surfaces
  • Use transparent variant when placing on photography or custom backgrounds
  • Maintain minimum 36px display size
  • Keep the amber origin dot visible at all sizes
  • Preserve the aspect ratio (always square)
  • Use SVG format for all digital use
✗ Don't
  • Don't recolour the M or D letterforms independently
  • Don't remove the axis lines or origin dot
  • Don't stretch or distort the mark
  • Don't display below 36px
  • Don't place on backgrounds that reduce contrast below legible
  • Don't add drop shadows, glows, or outer effects
  • Don't reproduce without permission
